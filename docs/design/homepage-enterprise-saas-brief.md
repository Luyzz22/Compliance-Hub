# Homepage enterprise SaaS brief

## Experience brief

For DACH governance, security and procurement leaders, this site helps them understand how Compliance Hub turns AI obligations into controlled, reviewable work.
It should make requesting a product briefing feel precise, low-risk and useful.
The strongest proof is the implemented tenant, evidence, audit, human-review and release-gate architecture documented in the repository and Trust Center.
The experience should feel exact, assured and operational, never templated or legally overconfident.
The visual premise is a governance ledger: every layer has a place, every decision leaves a trace.

## Mode and dials

- Primary mode: trust-first enterprise
- Supporting mode: product SaaS
- `DESIGN_VARIANCE: 5`
- `MOTION_INTENSITY: 4`
- `VISUAL_DENSITY: 5`
- Redesign mode: targeted evolution. Preserve information architecture, routes, contact intents, legal copy and owned hero assets.

## Audit before change

### Preserve

- Compliance Hub wordmark and established German terminology.
- Public navigation: Start, Trust Center, Kontakt.
- Three high-resolution governance images and accessible carousel behavior.
- Human-review and no-automatic-legal-decision qualifications.
- Public-site versus enterprise release boundary.
- Existing contact attribution and routes.

### Retire

- Centered statement followed by a second card-like hero.
- Three identical feature cards and repeated numbered section devices.
- Decorative status dots, excessive pills and broad glassmorphism.
- Text-only imitation logos for unverified integrations.
- Fake-precise dashboard metrics presented as product evidence.
- Repeated dark/light theme switching and excessive shadow elevation.

## Page narrative

1. State the operational outcome and show the governance mechanism in the first viewport.
2. Establish verified public-release and review properties without invented metrics.
3. Explain the path from scope to accountable decision.
4. Let visitors inspect the product logic without mistaking illustrative data for live customer evidence.
5. Show integration and deployment boundaries honestly.
6. Explain the enterprise release gate and route deeper review to the Trust Center.
7. Offer one consistent next action: Demo anfragen.

## Visual thesis

Enterprise assurance expressed as a precise, light governance ledger: cool neutral fields, ink typography, one GitHub-adjacent cobalt accent, sparse rules and image-led control-plane moments.

## Motion contract

- Carousel transitions explain the three governance layers.
- Control feedback confirms hover, focus, selected tabs and pause state.
- No scroll hijacking, pointer physics, parallax or decorative perpetual animation.
- Reduced-motion users receive immediate static states.

## Source influences

- GitHub: product demonstration early, compact navigation, strong reading order and evidence close to claims.
- Impeccable: one committed visual grammar, no generic card scaffolds and a bounded screenshot-based finish pass.
- Taste Skill: redesign audit, anti-template discipline, explicit design dials and no fabricated social proof.
- Google DESIGN.md: durable machine-readable tokens paired with human rationale and lintable contrast rules.
